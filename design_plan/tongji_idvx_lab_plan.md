# Design Plan for Tongji University's IDVX Lab

## Objective
To create a cohesive and modern visual identity for Tongji University's IDVX Lab, including a visual system, product packaging, and promotional materials. The design will align with a modern minimalist style, emphasizing clarity, functionality, and aesthetic appeal.

---

## Actionable Steps

### 1. Research and Discovery
- **Timeline**: 1 week
- **Actions**:
  1. Conduct a brand audit of Tongji University and IDVX Lab to understand existing visual elements.
  2. Research modern minimalist design trends in academic and research institutions.
  3. Identify key values, mission, and vision of the IDVX Lab to inform the design direction.
- **Deliverables**:
  - Mood boards with visual inspirations.
  - A summary document of key findings and design direction.

### 2. Develop the Visual System
- **Timeline**: 2 weeks
- **Actions**:
  1. Define a color palette that reflects the lab's identity and aligns with Tongji University's branding.
  2. Select typography that complements the minimalist style and ensures readability.
  3. Design a logo or refine the existing one to fit the new visual system.
  4. Create design motifs and patterns that can be used across various materials.
- **Deliverables**:
  - A comprehensive style guide including color palette, typography, logo usage, and design motifs.

### 3. Product Packaging Design
- **Timeline**: 2 weeks
- **Actions**:
  1. Identify the types of products that require packaging (e.g., lab kits, promotional items).
  2. Design packaging templates that incorporate the visual system.
  3. Prototype packaging designs for key products.
- **Deliverables**:
  - Packaging templates and prototypes.

### 4. Promotional Materials
- **Timeline**: 2 weeks
- **Actions**:
  1. Design templates for digital and print materials, including posters, flyers, and social media graphics.
  2. Create a presentation deck template for lab presentations.
  3. Develop a website mockup or landing page design that aligns with the visual system.
- **Deliverables**:
  - Digital and print templates.
  - Presentation deck template.
  - Website mockup or landing page design.

### 5. Review and Feedback
- **Timeline**: 1 week
- **Actions**:
  1. Present the designs to stakeholders for feedback.
  2. Iterate on the designs based on feedback.
- **Deliverables**:
  - Finalized designs ready for implementation.

---

## Resource Allocation

### Team
- **Designer**: Responsible for creating the visual system, packaging, and promotional materials.
- **Project Manager**: Oversees timelines and ensures alignment with objectives.
- **Stakeholders**: Provide feedback and approve designs.

### Tools
- **Design Software**: Adobe Creative Suite (Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign), Figma for digital designs.
- **Prototyping Tools**: Mockup templates, 3D rendering software for packaging.

## Success Metrics
- **Consistency**: All materials align with the visual system.
- **Stakeholder Approval**: Positive feedback from stakeholders.
- **Usability**: Templates and designs are easy to implement and adapt.
- **Aesthetic Appeal**: Designs reflect a modern minimalist style and resonate with the target audience.
