        // Reset heights on small screens

    const groupSize = 3; // Number of items in a group

    for (let i = 0; i < titles.length; i += groupSize) {

				<div class="elementor-widget-container">
									<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">ElliQ, an AI-powered companion designed to support older adults, was developed in partnership with Fuseproject and a team of aging and robotics specialists in 2016. The mission was clear: create a product that would enhance the lives of seniors aging in place, but the form factor and experience were uncharted territory.<br /><br /></span><span style="font-weight: 400;">The design process was a journey of discovery. Through research and interviews, we learned that seniors didn’t want a robot following them around. Instead, they preferred a stationary companion that acknowledged their presence, initiated conversations, and offered support—without becoming overly intrusive or emotionally dependent. Human connection remained paramount for them, and they desired a smart object that could engage them without trying to replace human interactions.</span></p>								</div>
				</div>
				</div>
		<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-4d39288 e-con-full e-flex e-con e-child" data-id="4d39288" data-element_type="container" data-e-type="container">
				<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-4c0006b mb-0 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="4c0006b" data-element_type="widget" data-e-type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
				<div class="elementor-widget-container">
									<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Fuseproject’s design for ElliQ was based on these insights. Rather than resembling a cute pet or toy, ElliQ was designed as a friendly object with two distinct parts: an emotive component with light and movement, and a screen for video calls, reading, and watching videos. This separation allowed users to feel they were interacting with a smart, helpful device rather than a dependent companion.</span></p><p> </p><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Launched just before COVID, ElliQ addressed the growing sense of isolation among seniors. Today, local governments in states like Florida, New York, and New Jersey are promoting ElliQ, making it available for free to seniors in need of support and companionship. ElliQ exemplifies how design and technology can empower older adults to maintain independence while fostering meaningful engagement.</span></p>								</div>
