IEEE VIS 2026

Promotional Visual System — A complete conference identity for the premier forum for advances in visualization and visual analytics. November 9–13, 2026 · Boston, Massachusetts.

This system applies Swiss/International Typographic Style principles — bold geometric sans-serif typography, strict grid alignment, high-contrast dark palette, and abstract geometric motifs — across 11 deliverables spanning print poster, digital social media, physical conference badges, and presentation templates. All elements derive from a single consistency anchor (the main poster) to ensure brand coherence at every scale and touchpoint.
