# Brand Lock: IEEE VIS 2026

## Mode
extension — The conference has an established official identity (logo, website theme, hex-pattern motif). The task is to extend this identity across a full promotional system (poster, social media, badges, PPT) while respecting IEEE brand guidelines.

## Found official sources
- https://ieeevis.org/year/2026/welcome — Official site with logo, dates, venue, Boston imagery
- https://ieeevis.org/year/2026/info/call-participation/call-for-participation/ — Six research areas, publication venue (TVCG)

## Existing identity handling

### Protected marks (do not replace, do not recreate)
- **IEEE VIS 2026 logo** — Official conference mark. Must appear on all deliverables. SVG source at `https://ieeevis.org/year/2026/assets/vis2026_logo.svg` (rasterization rejected by tool; use the official file directly in production).
- **IEEE master brand logo** — Protected trademark. Must appear per IEEE brand guidelines. White version at `https://ieeevis.org/year/2026/assets/theme/ieee-logo-white.svg`.
- **IEEE VGTC logo** — Co-organizer mark. White version at `https://ieeevis.org/year/2026/assets/theme/ieee-vgtc-logo-white.svg`.

### Existing visual motifs (reference, do not copy exactly)
- **Hex-pattern texture** — Used throughout the VIS 2026 website. Suggests data/visualization aesthetic. Can inspire original geometric patterns but should not be copied pixel-for-pixel.
- **Color palette** — Website uses dark backgrounds with accent colors. The design system should extend this palette consistently.
- **Boston skyline imagery** — Used in the official carousel. Can be referenced for Boston context but the poster should not simply reproduce the skyline photo.

### Past conference identities (learn from, do not duplicate)
- **VIS 2024 (Salt Lake City)** — Had its own distinct logo and merchandise system. Shows how each year creates unique identity while maintaining VIS brand continuity.
- **VIS 2025 (Vienna)** — Used isotype-based identity system. Demonstrates systematic approach to multi-touchpoint branding.

## Safe design directions
- Extend the existing IEEE VIS 2026 identity across all deliverables using a modular grid system
- Use minimalist geometric forms inspired by Swiss/International Typographic Style
- Employ bold, oversized sans-serif typography for headlines
- Create original geometric patterns inspired by (but not copying) the hex-pattern texture
- Use dark backgrounds with high-contrast bright accents
- Incorporate subtle Boston references without overwhelming the academic tone
- Design a systematic visual language that scales from A2 poster to social media to badge to PPT

## Do not duplicate
- The IEEE VIS 2026 logo — use the official file, never recreate it
- The IEEE master brand logo — protected trademark
- The IEEE VGTC logo — protected trademark
- The exact hex-pattern texture from the website — reference the aesthetic, create original variations
- Past conference logos (2024, 2025) — each year has its own identity
- Generic AI-generated conference poster clichés (gradient blobs, abstract tech circles, etc.)
